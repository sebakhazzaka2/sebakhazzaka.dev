The Problem

Managing multiple independent dental clinics on a single monolithic instance caused data isolation concerns and made custom updates risky. The administrative overhead for onboarding a new clinic was manually intensive and error-prone.

The Solution

Implemented a highly automated, containerized architecture. By leveraging Docker and a reverse proxy, we achieved complete physical isolation of databases and application instances per client while maintaining a single cohesive codebase.

Post-Mortem / Aprendizajes

Deploying a multi-tenant system on bare metal taught me the critical importance of declarative infrastructure and aggressive automation. Every manual step in onboarding a clinic was a potential point of failure that had to be scripted out.

Furthermore, managing technical debt requires rigorous documentation. Acknowledging a sub-optimal pattern is the first step to architecting its replacement.
